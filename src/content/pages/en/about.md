## Who we are

YESTOURS is the trading name of **YES Tourist Services Company Limited**, a licensed tour operator founded in 2017 and based in Da Nang. After close to ten years we work across three areas: bringing international visitors to central Vietnam, taking Vietnamese travellers to Korea and Japan, and running domestic programmes departing from Da Nang.

## Why travellers choose us

**Korean–Vietnamese management.** Our director is Mr SONG JAE NAM, a Korean national. That is more than a line on a licence. It means Korean visitors to Da Nang get an itinerary built by someone who understands what they are expecting, and Vietnamese groups heading to Korea get a schedule arranged the way people who live there actually travel — not one translated from somebody else's brochure.

**Based in Da Nang, not a branch office.** Our vehicles, our guides and our relationships with hotels and restaurants across the region are all here. When something goes wrong — a flight time changes, the weather turns, a traveller falls ill — the person fixing it is in the same city as the group.

**Nearly ten years on the same routes.** We have not tried to sell every destination in the world. Central Vietnam and Korea are the places we travel often enough to know which season to avoid, what time of day a site is worst, and when to tell a client honestly that an itinerary is trying to do too much.

## What we do

- **Inbound tours** — receiving international and Korean visitors in Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue and across central Vietnam.
- **Outbound tours** — Vietnamese groups to Korea and Japan, including visa guidance and application handling.
- **Domestic tours** — programmes departing Da Nang for Hue, Quang Binh, Quy Nhon and nearby provinces.
- **Corporate groups and MICE** — bespoke programmes designed around a company's schedule, group size and budget.
