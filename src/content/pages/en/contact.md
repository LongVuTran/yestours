You are welcome to contact our office in Da Nang directly, or send us a request through the form below.

To get you a useful answer the first time, please tell us **which service you need** (Ba Na Hills tickets or a hotel booking), **how many people**, **the date** and, for hotels, **the star rating (4 or 5)** and **the number of nights**. If your company needs an invoice, please say so and we will prepare it.

We reply during Vietnam business hours (GMT+7), usually within one working day. Requests sent over a weekend or public holiday are handled on the morning of the next working day.

If you are already in Da Nang and would rather talk in person, please arrange a time in advance so we can have someone available to receive you.
