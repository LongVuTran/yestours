This is the northern route we run most often for groups travelling up from Da Nang: company trips, association outings, families across three generations. Four days is the sensible length — enough for both Ha Long and Ninh Binh without a single four-in-the-morning start.

Unlike the version we sell to international visitors, this one cruises the bay by day and puts the group in a hotel in Bai Chay. Cabins on the boats are limited and hard to allocate by family; a hotel on shore makes triples and quads straightforward and gives the group somewhere to eat dinner together. For a large group it also costs less.

The Ha Long to Ninh Binh leg is about four hours and we drive it in the morning, so the group reaches the Trang An landing when the light has softened. The last day is deliberately light — Mua Cave and then the airport — because after three days most people's legs have had enough. Group size, hotel category and flight times are agreed before the contract is signed.
