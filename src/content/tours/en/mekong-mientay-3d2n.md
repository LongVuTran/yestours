The delta is the hardest region in Vietnam to sell on paper, because written down every stop sounds the same: a river, an orchard, a rowing boat. The difference is entirely in the timing. Reach a floating market at seven in the morning and all you find is a few boats selling soft drinks to tourists; reach it at five and you are in the middle of a working wholesale market, with watermelon and pineapple barges tied alongside each other transferring cargo.

So the second day starts very early, and we say so in advance rather than springing it on the group. In exchange, that afternoon is left almost empty — back to the hotel to rest, or a coffee looking out over the Hau River.

Three days is the minimum for this route out of Da Nang, because the flight and the drive down from Ho Chi Minh City take up most of the first day on their own. It works well for older travellers; the steps down to the boats are low and there is always someone to take an arm. Group size, hotel category and flight times are agreed before the contract is signed.
