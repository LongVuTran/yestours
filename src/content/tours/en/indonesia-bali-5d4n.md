Bali is not large — you can drive it north to south in about three hours — but the roads are narrow and often jammed, so the question is never how many places you can reach but in what order. This itinerary splits the island into three directions and takes one per day: Ubud in the middle, Bedugul to the north, Uluwatu to the south.

The two sunsets in the programme are deliberate. Tanah Lot and Uluwatu are both sea temples and both are at their best in the last forty minutes of the day; arrive at midday and you get hard light and crowds instead. The trade-off is two late returns to the hotel.

Bali is the one Hindu island in Indonesia and its temples are working places of worship, not museums. You will need to wear a sarong to enter — we provide them — and should stay out of the inner courtyard when a ceremony is underway.
