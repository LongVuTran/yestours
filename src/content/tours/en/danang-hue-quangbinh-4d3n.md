This route is for travellers who have already done Da Nang and Hoi An and want to go further north. Quang Binh is around seven hours by road from Da Nang, so four days is the minimum that lets you see Hue properly and still get into both major caves without spending a whole day in a seat.

Phong Nha and Paradise are two very different caves. Phong Nha is a river cave: you go in by boat and hear the paddles echoing off the roof. Paradise is dry, walked on a boardwalk under a ceiling as high as a twenty-storey building. We put both in the same day because they are close together and because each makes the other more impressive.

A seasonal note: Phong Nha Cave can close when the Son River runs high during the flood season, typically September to November. If that happens we will tell you in advance and substitute an equivalent site rather than simply dropping it from the programme.
