Three days and two nights is the standard shape of a Da Nang trip from Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City: fly out Friday evening, home Sunday afternoon, no leave required. This itinerary is built around exactly that rhythm.

We put Ba Na in the middle day and start early, because the cable car is the one bottleneck in the whole trip — arrive before nine and you walk straight on, arrive after ten and the queue can be long. The last day is kept light, just the Marble Mountains and Hoi An, so nobody arrives at the airport out of breath.

We book hotels on the My Khe beachfront rather than downtown, so that mornings and evenings you can simply walk out to the sea. Hotel category, flight times and group size are agreed when the contract is signed.
