Quy Nhon is about 300 kilometres south of Da Nang and still far quieter than Nha Trang or Da Nang itself. That is precisely the reason to go: clean beaches, cheap seafood, and enough space at Ky Co to find a stretch of sand to yourself.

Day two is the heart of the trip. Ky Co and Eo Gio are both on the Phuong Mai peninsula, so we spend the whole day out there rather than splitting them up. We aim to reach the jetty early, while the sea is still calm enough for a comfortable crossing.

The programme travels by road in both directions. If you would rather fly one leg into Phu Cat Airport to save time, we can rework the schedule — just raise it before the itinerary is finalised.
