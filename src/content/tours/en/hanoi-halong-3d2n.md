This is the most basic route through northern Vietnam and the one we run most often for first-time visitors. Three days is enough to see two very different faces of the country: a Hanoi that is crowded, loud and constantly in motion, and then a bay so quiet at night that you can hear the water against the hull.

We always build this trip around a night on board rather than a Ha Long day trip. A day trip gives you roughly four hours on the water, at exactly the hour when the bay is busiest. Staying out means you get the late afternoon and the early morning when there is almost nobody else around. The difference in cost is modest; the difference in the experience is not.

The Hanoi to Ha Long leg runs on the expressway and takes about two and a half hours, rather than the half-day drive it used to be. The itinerary suits couples, families and small groups. Cabin grade on the boat and hotel category in Hanoi are agreed before the contract is signed.
