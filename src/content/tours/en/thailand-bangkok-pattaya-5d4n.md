This is the easiest overseas trip we sell: a direct flight of about two hours from Da Nang, no visa required for Vietnamese passport holders on a short tourist stay, and food and drink in Thailand that costs very little. For a good number of our groups, this is the first trip abroad they have taken.

We drive straight to Pattaya on the first day rather than starting with a night in Bangkok. Suvarnabhumi Airport is on the eastern side of the city, so Pattaya is only about two hours further on; going into central Bangkok first and coming back out the next day costs the better part of a day in traffic. Bangkok is saved for the last two days, once the group has found its rhythm.

Thai temples require covered clothing: sleeves, trousers or skirts below the knee, and shoes off in the main hall. We remind everyone the day before Wat Arun and Wat Traimit, because some places will refuse entry rather than simply mention it. Group size, hotel category and departure dates are agreed before the contract is signed.
