Phu Quoc covers about 570 square kilometres and more than half of it is national park, so however much has gone up in the south, most of the island is still forest. This itinerary leans towards the sea and the island's working life rather than the attractions — we run a separate route for groups travelling with children.

Day two is the day that matters and we always place it early. The speedboat out to the An Thoi archipelago depends entirely on the sea state; scheduling it for day two means that if the water is rough there is still day three to swap into. The calmest months are roughly November through April.

The fish sauce house tends to get skipped because of the smell, but it is what kept this island going for more than a century before there was an international airport. Standing among barrels taller than you are while someone explains why only anchovies landed the same day will do is one of the better half hours of the trip. Group size and hotel category are agreed before the contract is signed.
