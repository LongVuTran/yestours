Nha Trang is the easiest beach city in central Vietnam to travel in: the airport is 35 km from the centre, the hotels sit directly on the seafront road, and almost everything worth seeing is either within about 10 km or out in the bay. Four days here means no hotel change at all.

We put the sea and the islands on two consecutive days and leave the land-based sights until afterwards. The reason is practical: boats out to the bay depend on the weather, so if day two is rough there is still day three to swap into without wrecking the programme. The mud bath is scheduled for the end of a day for the same sort of reason — after hours in the sun and wind it is the obvious thing to want.

Po Nagar deserves more time than most visitors give it. It is not a ruin: Cham communities from Ninh Thuan and Binh Thuan still travel here to make offerings, and the stone goddess remains in place in the main tower. If the group arrives during a ceremony, your guide will point out which areas call for quiet. Group size and hotel category are agreed before the contract is signed.
