Taiwan covers a lot of ground for a short trip, because the island is only around 400 km long and the motorway network is very good. Five days is enough to work from Taipei in the north down to Nantou in the middle and back without a single day spending more than about three hours on the coach.

Day two is the fullest in the programme: the city in the morning, then the hills at Jiufen and Shifen in the afternoon. We combine them deliberately, because they lie in the same direction and splitting them costs another half day of driving. The trade-off is a late return to the hotel that evening.

Taiwan gets a lot of rain, particularly in the north-eastern hills around Jiufen, and typhoon season runs roughly July to September. Pack a light rain jacket. We prepare and submit the Taiwan visa applications on your behalf; you supply a valid passport and the documents on a checklist we send in advance. Group size, hotel category and departure dates are fixed when the contract is signed.
