This is the Phu Quoc itinerary for groups travelling with children. If you have young children or grandchildren along, most of the time goes to the south of the island — the cable car, the water park, Sunset Town — because those are the places where children can spend a whole day and the adults still have somewhere shaded to sit.

We keep half a day for what the island actually does for a living: a pepper farm and a fish sauce house. Phu Quoc was known for both long before there was a cable car, and the fish sauce house is usually what the adults in the group remember longest, however unpromising it sounds written down.

The Hon Thom cable car can be suspended in high wind, most often between June and September. If it is down that day, we switch to a speedboat trip around the smaller islands of the An Thoi archipelago and reshuffle the days that follow. Group size, hotel category and flight times are agreed before the contract is signed.
