This is two countries in one trip, which only works because Singapore and Malaysia are joined by road. We fly into Changi, travel overland through Malacca to Kuala Lumpur and fly home from there — no doubling back, which saves close to a day against returning to the same airport.

Day three is the long one: land border formalities, a stop in Malacca, then the run up to Kuala Lumpur. The wait at the Woodlands–Johor crossing is unpredictable — sometimes quick, sometimes an hour or more — so we leave early and put nothing else in that evening.

The two countries differ more than the distance between them suggests: Singapore tidy and expensive, Malaysia larger and considerably cheaper, and Malacca carrying the marks of three colonial periods layered on top of each other. Bring both currencies. Group size, hotel category and departure dates are agreed before the contract is signed.
