Four days in Ho Chi Minh City divides cleanly into three parts: the colonial centre, two sites about the war, and a day out in the delta. That structure is deliberate, so that nobody leaves with only one impression of the city.

Day two is the difficult one. Putting Cu Chi and the War Remnants Museum in the same day is a choice; splitting them across two days, in our experience, leaves both feeling unfinished. Your guide will give you the events and the context, and you can ask anything, including the hard questions. That evening is left completely free.

The stretch of tunnel open to visitors at Cu Chi has been widened from its original dimensions, but it is still low and dark; anyone uncomfortable in enclosed spaces can take the surface path and rejoin the group at the far end. The run down to My Tho and Ben Tre is about ninety minutes each way, enough to see the delta while still sleeping in the city. Group size and hotel category are agreed before the contract is signed.
