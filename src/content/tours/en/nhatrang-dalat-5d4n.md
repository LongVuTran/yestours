This route puts two climates into one trip. On the morning of day three you are swimming at Nha Trang; by that evening you need a jacket in Da Lat. The Khanh Le pass is what joins them, and for a lot of groups the drive itself turns out to be the part they talk about afterwards, not the destinations.

We fly into Cam Ranh and out of Lien Khuong rather than doubling back. That saves the better part of a day sitting in a coach, but it means two one-way fares that move independently, so this itinerary needs booking earlier than most. The sooner you fix your dates, the easier the seats are to hold.

Day three is deliberately the lightest day in the programme. The Khanh Le pass is one bend after another and a fair number of people arrive feeling it, so that afternoon is nothing but check-in and rest, with the night market saved for later. If anyone in the group gets travel sick, tell us in advance and we will seat them at the front. Group size, hotel category and flight times are agreed before the contract is signed.
