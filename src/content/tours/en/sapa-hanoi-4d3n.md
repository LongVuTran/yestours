This is not the standard Sa Pa route. We leave out Fansipan and Cat Cat village — the two stops almost every group makes — in order to spend the time on Y Ty and Bac Ha, which are what people ask about on a second visit to the region.

Y Ty is the point of the trip. The road in is about 70 km of mountain switchbacks and takes close to three hours each way, so it needs a whole day; in return it is the least visited terrace country in Lao Cai, with Ha Nhi villages whose earth walls are a hand's span thick. Fog at Y Ty is dense and arrives without warning, and there are days when you see nothing at all. We say so in advance.

The last day depends on Bac Ha market, so departures have to fall on a Thursday for the group to be there on Sunday morning. If your dates cannot work that way, we switch to Can Cau market on Saturday and adjust the rest. There is a great deal of winding road on this trip, so anyone badly affected by travel sickness should think it over. Group size, accommodation standard and flight times are agreed before the contract is signed.
