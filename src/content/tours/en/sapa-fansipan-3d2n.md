Sa Pa sits at around 1,500 metres and can be cold, fogged in or drizzling in the middle of summer. That is the first thing we tell international visitors before they come, because plenty of people arrive in Vietnam with nothing but short sleeves in the bag. Bring a windproof jacket and shoes with grip.

We schedule Fansipan for the last day rather than the first. The summit is often in cloud and the cloud here changes by the hour; putting it last means we have two nights to watch the forecast and some flexibility to move the cable car time if the morning comes in thick. Nobody can guarantee the view from the top, and we do not promise one.

Cat Cat and Ta Van are villages where people live, not displays. Please ask before photographing anyone, particularly children, and if you want to buy woven cloth, buy it directly from the woman who made it. Your guide will interpret. Group size and hotel category are agreed before the contract is signed.
