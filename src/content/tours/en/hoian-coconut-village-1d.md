Most Hoi An day trips stop at the old town. We add the Cam Thanh water palm forest in the morning because it is the part of Hoi An that visitors rarely hear about: a tidal wetland less than ten minutes from the old quarter where people still make a living from the water.

The afternoon belongs to the old town, and we deliberately do not bring the group back early. Hoi An is at its best after sunset, when the street lighting goes off and silk lanterns come on along both banks of the Hoai River. You get a few unhurried hours there rather than being chivvied back to the coach.

The trip runs out and back from Da Nang in one day, with no hotel change. If your group includes small children or older travellers who would rather not be paddled far, the guide will arrange a shorter, gentler route.
