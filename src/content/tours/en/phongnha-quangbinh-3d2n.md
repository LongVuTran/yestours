This programme does one thing: caves. We drop Hue and the roadside stops that the four-day Da Nang – Hue – Quang Binh route takes in, and in exchange you get two full days inside the national park rather than one rushed afternoon.

Over three hundred caves have been surveyed at Phong Nha and only a handful are open to general visitors. The three in this itinerary are three different kinds of cave: Phong Nha is a river cave you enter by boat; Paradise is a dry cave, walked on boardwalk and cool all year; the Dark Cave you swim into and wade through mud, and your clothes will not survive dry.

Day two is the active day — bring a change of clothes, sandals with a heel strap and a waterproof pouch for your phone. The rainy season in Quang Binh runs roughly September to November, when the Son River can rise high enough for the authorities to close Phong Nha cave. We always hold an alternative, but we flag it in advance so you can weigh up the season. Group size and accommodation standard are agreed before the contract is signed.
