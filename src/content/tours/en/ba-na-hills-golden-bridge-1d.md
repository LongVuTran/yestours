Ba Na is the easiest day trip out of Da Nang and by far the most requested. The drive to the cable car station takes about 45 minutes, but by the time the cabin reaches the top the temperature has dropped close to ten degrees and you are standing in a hill station the French began building in 1919.

The Golden Bridge is why most people book this trip. We aim to get the group onto the bridge as early in the day as possible: after ten o'clock it fills up and the cloud usually burns off. If photography matters to you, tell your guide in advance and we will plan the morning around it.

The trip runs out and back in a single day, so there is no hotel change. It works well for anyone with only one free day in Da Nang. Most of the summit is paved and served by lifts and internal shuttles, so children and older travellers manage it comfortably.
