Zhangjiajie is a walking trip, not a resort trip. Over five days you cover a lot of ground on foot, move constantly between cable cars and lifts, and spend a good deal of time on paths that run along the edge of a cliff. In return you get one of the few places where photographs genuinely fail to convey the thing itself: several thousand rock columns, many over two hundred metres tall, standing straight up out of the forest.

We place Fenghuang in the middle of the itinerary rather than at the end. After two solid days on the mountains, an evening walking slowly along the Tuojiang River is a break the group needs before the flight home. The drive between the two is about four hours, and we schedule it for the afternoon so you never lose the best light of the day to a coach.

Weather here changes quickly and the summits ice over in winter. Bring walking shoes with grip, a windproof jacket and a hat. We prepare and submit the Chinese visa applications on your behalf; you supply a valid passport and the documents on a checklist we send in advance. Group size, hotel category and departure dates are fixed when the contract is signed.
